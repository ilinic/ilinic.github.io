3. Projects and engineering work

Examples across AI tools, enterprise platforms, web and mobile applications, industrial control, 3D engineering software, video streaming, security, scientific computing and education. Each entry describes the work and the technologies involved.

  1. ResearchTube — connecting ChatGPT with YouTube and the web

    Much of YouTube's knowledge is inaccessible to ChatGPT. ResearchTube adds video search, comments, subtitles and downloads, automatically sends frames and storyboards into chat, explores sites behind your login, and offers many other tools.

    I designed and built this free, open-source extension to deeply explore LLM integration with external tools, including browser automation, media processing and other AI tools.

    GitHub

  2. Mobile HMI — remote rectification control

    Monitor temperatures, follow process trends and operate industrial rectification units remotely through the equipment manufacturer's industrial cloud.

    I chose Dart and Flutter for a shared codebase targeting iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and Linux. Starting with limited experience in the stack, I learned Dart fundamentals, designed the architecture and built the application using modern AI development tools and my background in industrial software.

  3. Unimarket — enterprise procurement platform

    I spent over five years as a full-stack developer at Unimarket, an enterprise procurement platform serving hundreds of thousands of concurrent users across the United States, New Zealand and Australia.

    My work spanned user interfaces, backend services, business logic, data access and integrations within a high-traffic microservices architecture on Google Cloud, alongside production maintenance and troubleshooting.

    Technologies: Java, Spring / Spring Boot, Apache Wicket, Google Cloud Platform, Pub/Sub and APIs.

    Unimarket website

  4. Inventory Mini — stock management from a smartphone

    I built Inventory Mini for a small manufacturer to keep stock records current from a smartphone. A lightweight PHP / MySQL web application works with an Android app: scan a product QR code, record quantity changes, and check stock levels and movement history.

    Product lists can be loaded and updated from Excel-exported CSV files, making initial setup and adding new items straightforward.

    GitHub

  5. PROLITH — lithography simulation and numerical research

    I led research and software development through Mirantis for KLA-Tencor's PROLITH lithography simulator, helping turn physical models into software for investigating semiconductor manufacturing processes.

    The work covered numerical methods and solvers for partial differential equations, with a focus on algorithm stability, numerical accuracy and validation through computational experiments.

    Technologies: C/C++, Mathcad and numerical modelling.

    PROLITH on the KLA website

  6. AMDD — automated photomask defect assessment

    My research and development team also contributed to KLA-Tencor's AMDD (Automated Mask Defect Disposition System). It used PROLITH-based simulation to predict how photomask defects would affect the pattern printed on a wafer and help determine whether a mask was fit for use.

    By comparing defective and reference images under production exposure conditions, the system supported consistent, automated defect assessment for semiconductor manufacturing.

    AMDD — published product evaluation

  7. Ceres PLC — rectification automation

    I developed control logic and an embedded HMI for industrial rectification equipment, covering heaters, pumps, temperature and pressure monitoring, and equipment communication.

    The compact controller's small, low-resolution screen and limited graphics toolkit shaped the interface: a simple equipment diagram, essential readings and direct controls operated with physical buttons.

    Technologies: CODESYS, ST function blocks, Modbus.

  8. CERES 4 — Linux control application for rectification

    I developed a Java application for Linux to operate a multi-column rectification plant. It communicates with low-level automation over Modbus, combining an operator HMI with automatic operating modes, built-in protection logic and automatic shutdown.

    An extensive parameter archive and trend charts support process review and troubleshooting, while Google Firebase provides cloud telemetry.

    Technologies: Java, Linux, Google Firebase, Modbus, CODESYS, ST function blocks.

  9. CERES 2.1 — brewery automation

    Technologies: Java, Swing, C/C++, STM32, Modbus and SQLite.
