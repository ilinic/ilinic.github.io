# Distributed electrical substation control

The work description and technologies follow Artem's account: application and embedded development for distributed electrical substations, PowerManager interfaces and communications, backup power control and battery charging logic; Delphi, C++, IAR and client-server applications. ## Product identification and link

PowerManagerII is documented as Eaton's remote management software for DC power systems, including site maps, alarm/event logs and remote battery testing. It fits the product context Artem recalled. The portfolio retains his general PowerManager name rather than attributing his work to a specific release.
