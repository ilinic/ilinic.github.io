# AMDD illustrations and description

Artem states that his research and development group also developed AMDD for KLA-Tencor. The portfolio describes the group's contribution without claiming authorship of the published illustrations.

AMDD means Automated Mask Defect Disposition System. Its purpose and use of PROLITH technology are documented in the production evaluation:
https://www.vde-verlag.de/proceedings-de/563032010.html

## Public technical illustrations

Source: William B. Howard and Chris A. Mack (KLA-Tencor), "Accurate Aerial Image Simulation Using High-Resolution Reticle Inspection Images", Proceedings of SPIE 5835, 2005, DOI 10.1117/12.637289.
Author-hosted paper: https://www.lithoguru.com/scientist/litho_papers/2005_148_Accurate%20aerial%20image%20simulation%20using%20high-resolution%20reticle%20inspection%20images.pdf

- `defect-comparison.png`: Figure 3, page 5 of the PDF (printed page 93). Reference and defective inspection images, aerial images and the analysis region.
- `contour-analysis.png`: Figure 4 on the same page. Reference and test resist contours and edge displacement vectors.

These are figure-region renderings from the original PDF using Poppler, without redrawing, generated content or changed scientific data.
